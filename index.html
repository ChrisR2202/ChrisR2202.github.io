    How to cook bacon

    Ingredients

  • raw bacon
  • pan
  • oven

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees farenheit.
  2. line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil and lay bacon strips on it.
  3. place sheet of bacon in oven for 15-20 minutes.
  4. remove the pan from the oven and use tongs to transer bacon. let it cool and enjoy.
